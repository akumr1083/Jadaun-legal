01 The Firm

A practice built on the record,
not on rhetoric.

Jadaun Legal delivers strategic, client-focused representation. Led by Advocate Abhay Singh, the firm pairs deep legal expertise with precise analytical thinking to navigate complex judicial processes.

We are built on a foundation of integrity, professionalism and a commitment to protecting the rights of individuals and organisations — from constitutional courts to regulatory forums. Every matter is treated as a structure to be understood before it is argued.

02 Founder

Abhay Singh Jadaun

Advocate · Founder

Advocate Abhay Singh Jadaun practises before the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India, with experience representing government organisations, corporations and private clients before various courts and tribunals. He serves as a panel advocate for several Rajasthan government bodies.

He provides legal advisory services in corporate, contractual and regulatory matters. With an LL.M. from the University of Delhi, his work spans legal drafting, research, litigation, arbitration and legal administration.
