02 What we look for

Serious about the craft.

  • A strong record of research and writing — clarity matters more than volume.
  • Curiosity about procedure, evidence and the record, not only the headline issue.
  • Care with deadlines, filings and correspondence; the details are the work.
  • Discretion with client information and mature judgement under pressure.
  • Willingness to appear, argue and take responsibility as confidence grows.
  • For internships: a genuine interest in litigation and a clear indication of your available dates.

How to apply

  1. Email your CV and a short covering note to advabhaysingh07@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Careers — [role or internship] and tell us your availability.
  3. If your profile fits, we will write back to arrange a conversation. Applications are kept confidential.

We review every application ourselves. Jadaun Legal does not charge any fee at any stage of recruitment, and never asks for payment or original documents before an offer.

Ready to apply?

Send us your CV and a short note about what you're looking for. We'd love to hear from you. Please do not send confidential or privileged material with an application.
